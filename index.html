About 4s Group

Reliable, High-Quality Solutions Tailored to Your Needs

We are a dedicated steel fabrication, construction, and property maintenance company committed to delivering reliable, high-quality solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

Our wide range of services includes precision steel fabrication, 4View fencing, powder coating, galvanising, renovations, general maintenance, waterproofing, garden layout, and irrigation systems. We take pride in delivering quality workmanship, dependable service, and practical solutions tailored to our clients’ exact requirements.

Our Services Breakdown

Explore comprehensive technical details, typical job assignments, and specialized solutions for each service area.

01

Precision Steel Fabrication

We design, cut, weld, and assemble custom structural steel systems engineered for industrial strength and architectural precision. Whether building heavy steel frames or decorative gate structures, our fabrication meets structural safety standards.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Custom Driveway & Pedestrian Steel Gates
  • Structural Support Beams & I-Beams
  • Industrial Staircases, Handrails & Balustrades
  • Custom Trailer & Machinery Frame Builds

Specifications & Materials:

  • Mild Steel, Stainless Steel & Aluminum
  • MIG / TIG / Arc Precision Welding
  • On-site Measurement & Professional Fitting
02

4View High-Security Fencing

4View fencing combines maximum anti-climb, anti-cut security with unobstructed aesthetic views. Ideal for residential estates, commercial boundaries, and perimeter protection in Western Cape environments.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Clear-Vu Style High-Density Mesh Installation
  • Perimeter Gate Integration & Locking Mechanisms
  • Spike Tops & Anti-Intrusion Add-ons
  • Estate & Commercial Boundary Fencing

Key Benefits:

  • Anti-Cut & Anti-Climb Mesh Spacing
  • Corrosion-Resistant Coating Options
  • High Aesthetic Appeal with Zero Line-of-Sight Block
03

Industrial Powder Coating

Our electrostatic powder coating process delivers a smooth, uniform finish that is significantly tougher than conventional wet paint. It protects metal surfaces against UV rays, chipping, chemical exposure, and rust.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Outdoor Patio Furniture Refinishing
  • Steel Gate, Railing & Window Frame Powder Coating
  • Automotive Components & Rim Refurbishment
  • Industrial Equipment & Enclosure Finishing

Finish Options:

  • Matte, Satin, Gloss, and Textured Finishes
  • Wide Spectrum of Custom Colors
  • Thermal Cured for Maximum Surface Longevity
04

Galvanising Renovations

Coastal Western Cape weather causes rapid rust on untreated metals. We specialize in hot-dip galvanising restoration, stripped metal treatment, and zinc-rich protective coatings to double the lifespan of metal assets.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Rust Removal & Metal Surface Sandblasting
  • Hot-Dip Galvanising Treatments
  • Cold-Galvanising Zinc Primer Application
  • Corroded Structural Beam Rehabilitation

Why It Matters:

  • Prevents Salt-Air Corrosion Damage
  • Restores Structural Load Integrity
  • Reduces Future Maintenance Expenses
05

General Maintenance & Renovations

A full-spectrum facility and property upkeep service for homeowners, body corporates, and industrial facilities. We handle everything from minor structural repairs to complete property upgrades.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Residential & Commercial Building Painting
  • Paving Repairs & Boundary Wall Fixing
  • Roof Guttering & Structural Timber Works
  • Facility Upkeep & Wear-and-Tear Repairs

Execution Promise:

  • Supervised On-site Teams
  • Clean Workspaces Upon Completion
  • Scheduled Maintenance Contracts Available
06

Garden Layout & Irrigation

Transforming outdoor areas into sustainable landscape environments paired with water-saving automated irrigation systems tailored specifically for South African weather.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Automatic Pop-Up Sprinkler Installations
  • Drip Line System Setup for Flowerbeds & Trees
  • Garden Earthworks & Sodding/Lawn Laying
  • Irrigation Timer & Pump Repairs

Efficiency Standards:

  • Smart Rain Sensor Integration
  • Optimized Head-to-Head Water Coverage
  • Water-Wise Landscaping Design
07

Structural Waterproofing

Prevent costly water damage, damp, and structural degradation with our long-lasting waterproofing membrane applications for roofs, basements, and retaining walls.

Exact Jobs Included:

  • Torch-On Bitumen Membrane Roofing
  • Balcony & Terrace Liquid Waterproofing
  • Parapet Wall Sealant & Flashings
  • Rising Damp Treatments & Concrete Sealing

Protection Level:

  • Heavy Rain & Standing Water Proofing
  • Flexible Elastomeric Coatings
  • Guaranteed Workmanship Seal
